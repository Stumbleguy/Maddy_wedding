Our Story

It started the way most good things do — quietly, without any warning that it would matter. A conversation here, a shared joke there, and somewhere along the way, two lives that were doing just fine on their own started to feel a little better together.

What we found in each other wasn't a grand plan — it was patience, the kind of laughter that makes long days shorter, and the comfort of knowing someone is genuinely on your side. Slowly, "we should talk again sometime" became "I can't imagine not talking to you," and that became the easiest decision either of us has ever made.

Now, we're ready for the next part — and we'd love nothing more than to have the people who've shaped our lives sitting right there with us when it happens.
